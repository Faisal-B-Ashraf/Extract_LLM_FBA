3.2.1. Information Richness Analysis

The complex pipeline achieved high information completeness across all extractions (n=50), with 70% (35/50) classified as Full, 6% (3/50) as Value-only, and 24% (12/50) as Wrong (Fig. 6). Analysis by extraction pattern revealed 16 Constant, 20 Seasonal, and 14 Conditional cases. Among correct extractions (n=38), 92% (35/38) achieved Full completeness, demonstrating that when the pipeline identifies the correct value, it typically captures comprehensive regulatory context including dates, conditions, and operational constraints. Seasonal extractions exhibited consistent Full completeness (14/20, 70%), Conditional extractions achieved 86% Full completeness (12/14), while Constant extractions showed 56% Full completeness (9/16) with remaining cases split between Value-only and Wrong classifications. Document type analysis showed that FERC licenses comprised 88% (44/50) of all extractions, with USACE manuals contributing 12% (6/50).
